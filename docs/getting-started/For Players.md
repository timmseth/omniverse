# For Players

You need a DM, a character and twenty minutes. The Omniverse supplies the world.

## What you are joining
A **universe** (Tropelandia is the first: a fantasy parody played with a straight face) with its own towns, factions, dungeons and history. Your table plays in an **instance**: a private timeline forked from the universe's canon, where your actions are logged and nothing you do changes the shared world unless your DM proposes it and the universe's maintainer agrees (a **canon merge**). So: do anything. The world will have a workable answer.

## Making a character
Each universe names its ruleset in `Multiverse/<Universe>/_universe.md`. Tropelandia uses D&D 5e. Build a character under that system, or take a pre-generated one: the [Starter Kit](../../Multiverse/Tropelandia/Instances/starter-kit/_instance.md) has five, each with a reason to be in the tavern tonight.

Your character is a `person` note in the instance's `Overlay/` or `Pregens/` folder, with `player_character: true`. Your DM (or the DM skill) writes it; you only need to know your sheet.

## What to read before session one
Nothing, strictly. If you want flavour: the universe note, the town you start in (in Tropelandia, probably [Barrowdale](../../Multiverse/Tropelandia/Material%20Plane/Gleamhaven/Mildew%20Sun/Tropos/Mainland/Gently%20Haunted%20Marsh/Duchy%20of%20Mildew/Dampshire/Barrowdale/Barrowdale.md)), and the [Tavern Rumours](../../Multiverse/Tropelandia/Tables/Tavern%20Rumours.md) table. Do not read dungeons; that is the DM's side of the screen.

## During play
- Secrets stay secret: notes marked `visibility: secret` and anything under `dm_notes` are the DM's.
- What happens is logged as **events** in the session note. If you do something memorable, it is in the log with your name on it.
- Levels, loot and consequences follow the ruleset. The Omniverse adds persistence: grudges, rivalries and debts outlast the session.

## After the campaign
If your table did something the world should remember, your DM proposes a canon merge. Merged events carry the instance's name and the players' handles in the universe's history. That is how you get into the lore.
