# Genesis Session Handouts

Print these or put them on a shared screen. They go with [Running a Genesis Session](Running%20a%20Genesis%20Session.md), [Running a Convocation](Running%20a%20Convocation.md) and [the Gameplay Loop](../concepts/The%20Gameplay%20Loop.md). Sections 1 to 9 are for the players; 10 to 17 are for the Scribe.

---

## 1. Rules card: You are a god

You are one of the gods who make this world. Tonight you take turns creating it, one piece at a time. Everything you make costs Entropy, and before the night ends the gods must spend that debt on the Doom the world will face. Then you hide a relic for your future mortal self, and later you play that mortal, who remembers none of it, and goes to find the relic.

**Every turn is an Act. In order:**
1. **Declare** the size and the thing. The size is the cost.
2. **Speak the Word**: one or two sentences, in character, saying it into being.
3. **Anchor and Mark**: what it connects to, and how your Domain touches it.
4. **Yes-and**: other gods may each add one detail (two adds at most).
5. **The Note**: the Scribe writes it down (a name, a line, a useful detail).
6. **The Tally**: the Scribe adds the cost to the debt, out loud.

**Sizes and costs**
- **The World** (everyone together, once): 5.
- **Grand** (3): a plane, continent, a people, a law of reality.
- **Middling** (2): a region, country, city, faction, monster, era, language, holiday.
- **Small** (1): a person, item, place within a place, rumour, song, grudge.
- Making your god, hiding your relic, and your Proverb cost nothing.

**Rounds**
- Apotheosis: make your god, say your First Word, make the World together.
- Round 1: everyone makes something Grand.
- Middle rounds: Middling or Small. Make at least one Small thing before we finish.
- Last round: hide your **Relic** (secret) and say a **Proverb** (public).
- Reckoning: spend the debt on the Doom.

**Rules of the table**
- **Yes, and.** Never delete or contradict. If creations clash, both are true somewhere.
- **Anchor everything.** No floating ideas.
- **Mark everything.** Your Domain leaves a fingerprint.
- **Deals, not force.** You cannot stop a god from creating. You can Petition: offer two of your Weaving Acts for one of theirs, or any price you both like. They may say no; then you add a Caveat (a cost or limit) and the thing still happens.
- **Creation costs.** The debt is public. Nobody is at fault for it, and nobody may refuse it.
- **The Veil.** When the Ledger is sealed, you know only what your mortal knows.

## 2. Loop card

```
Apotheosis → Genesis → Reckoning → Relics → Incarnation
     ↑                                            ↓
Convocation ← Retirement ← Long Game ← Doom ← Relic Quest
```

| Phase | You are | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Apotheosis | a god | Make your god. Free. |
| Genesis | a god | Build the world by turns. Every creation adds Entropy. |
| Reckoning | a god | Spend the debt on the Doom. |
| Relics | a god | Hide one relic for your future mortal. Free. |
| Incarnation | a player | Make your avatar. The Veil comes down. |
| Relic Quest | an avatar | Recover the relics; yours wakes in your hands. |
| The Doom | an avatar | Join the relics and turn the Doom into something the world can live with. |
| Long Game | an avatar | Play on in the world you saved. |
| Retirement | a player | End your avatar: reborn, new chosen, return, or ascend. |
| Convocation | a god | Read the Ledger, settle Pacts, build more, owe more, skip time, hide new relics. Repeat. |

A base way, not the right way. Every phase has options; ask the Scribe.

## 4. Act Cards: prompts when you are stuck

### Grand (3)
- A continent shaped by something that happened to it.
- A people who exist because of a single choice.
- A law: what magic costs, what death means, what happens to promises.
- A great force: a sea, a storm, a hunger, a quiet.
- A second sky, or a place beyond the sky.
- A wound in the world that has not healed.
- A thing every living creature has in common.

### Middling (2)
- A city that exists for a strange reason.
- A country and the one thing it will never forgive.
- A faction with a noble goal and a bad method.
- A religion that worships the wrong god (maybe yours).
- A monster species and why it is hated or protected.
- A war, a famine, a miracle: something that began an era.
- A language with one word that cannot be translated.
- A holiday and the story everyone gets wrong.

### Small (1)
- A person with a want, a fear and a secret.
- A relic and what it cost.
- A tavern, tower, tree or ruin with a story.
- A rumour that is half true.
- A song, recipe or custom.
- A grudge between two families.
- An animal that matters for a reason.
- A door that should stay shut.

### Relics (secret; free; anchored to something already made)
- A blade, ring, key, lantern, bell or book that knows its owner.
- A person who will recognise you by something you do not remember.
- A place that is waiting for you.
- A debt that belongs to you from before.
- A truth about how a famous thing really happened.

### Proverbs (said aloud; one sentence mortals say)
- "Never trust a tide that comes in twice."
- "Everyone owes the Auditor."
- (Write your own: what do ordinary people say about the world you made?)

## 10. Scribe: the Opening (read aloud)

> Before anything, there is a table and nothing on it.
> No light, no ground, no names. A space the size of a held breath.
> Then someone says a word, and the space has a shape.
> Everything you make will leak. That is not your fault; it is what making is.
> You have been here since before there were hours. Tonight you decide what comes next, and what it costs.

Then run Apotheosis: God Sheets, the First Word, the World. Start the clock at the first Act.

## 14. Scribe: the Script

The whole procedure as the questions you ask. New players never need to hold the rules; you hold them.

**Apotheosis (each god)**
1. "What are you the god of? Two words at most."
2. "What do mortals call you?"
3. "What mark do you leave on things?"
4. "How do you speak?" (Ask them to say something in that voice.)
5. "Before anything existed, what was already true?" (The First Word.)
6. Then, going round: "Give me one line of the World."

**Every Act (in order)**
1. "Grand, Middling or Small? What is it?"
2. "Say it into being." (One or two sentences, in character.)
3. "What does it touch?" (Anchor.)
4. "Where's your fingerprint on it?" (Mark.)
5. "Anyone yes-and?" (Two adds at most.)
6. "Name it." Write it down, then say the tally out loud: "That's 3. We owe 21."

**When someone hesitates:** "Want a card?" (Act Cards.) "Small things are cheap and count."
**When someone wants to stop another god:** "Make them an offer. They can say no."
**When creations clash:** "Both are true. Tell me how."
**Every few Acts:** say the debt out loud.

**Relics (last round):** "Hide something for your future self. What is it, what does it do alone, what does it add when joined, where is it, how will you find it?" Collect the slip face down.

**Reckoning (Council):** read the sore spots, then "What's the scale?", "What form does it take?", "What else?" until the debt is spent. Then "What do mortals think is coming?"

**Incarnation (each player):** "Who are you?" "What pull do you feel and can't explain?" (Echo.) Hand them their Fate and Lead. "Why are you at the Gathering?"
