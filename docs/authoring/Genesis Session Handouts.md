# Genesis Session Handouts

Print these or put them on a shared screen. They go with [Running a Genesis Session](Running%20a%20Genesis%20Session.md). Sections 1 to 6 are for the players; 7 and 8 are for the Scribe.

---

## 1. Rules card: You are a god

You are one of the gods who make this world. Tonight you take turns creating it, one piece at a time. Later you will play a mortal who is part of you and remembers none of it.

**Every turn is an Act. In order:**
1. **Declare** the size and the thing.
2. **Speak the Word**: one or two sentences, in character, saying it into being.
3. **Anchor and Mark**: what it connects to, and how your Domain touches it.
4. **Yes-and**: other gods may each add one detail (two adds at most).
5. **The Note**: the Scribe writes it down (a name, a line, a useful detail).

**Sizes**
- **Grand**: a plane, world, continent, a people, a law of reality.
- **Middling**: a region, country, city, faction, monster, era, language, holiday.
- **Small**: a person, relic, place within a place, rumour, song, grudge.

**Rounds**
- Round 1: everyone makes something Grand.
- Middle rounds: Middling or Small. Make at least one Small thing before we finish.
- Last round: plant a secret **Seed** and say a public **Proverb**.

**Rules of the table**
- **Yes, and.** Never delete or contradict. If creations clash, both are true somewhere.
- **Anchor everything.** No floating ideas.
- **Mark everything.** Your Domain leaves a fingerprint.
- **Deals, not force.** You cannot stop a god from creating. You can Petition: offer two of your Weaving Acts for one of theirs, or any price you both like. They may say no; then you add a Caveat (a cost or limit) and the thing still happens.
- **The Veil.** When the Ledger is sealed, you know only what your mortal knows.

## 3. Act Cards: prompts when you are stuck

### Grand
- A world with one impossible feature.
- A continent shaped by something that happened to it.
- A people who exist because of a single choice.
- A law: what magic costs, what death means, what happens to promises.
- A great force: a sea, a storm, a hunger, a quiet.
- A second sky, or a place beyond the sky.
- A wound in the world that has not healed.
- A thing every living creature has in common.

### Middling
- A city that exists for a strange reason.
- A country and the one thing it will never forgive.
- A faction with a noble goal and a bad method.
- A religion that worships the wrong god (maybe yours).
- A monster species and why it is hated or protected.
- A war, a famine, a miracle: something that began an era.
- A language with one word that cannot be translated.
- A holiday and the story everyone gets wrong.

### Small
- A person with a want, a fear and a secret.
- A relic and what it cost.
- A tavern, tower, tree or ruin with a story.
- A rumour that is half true.
- A song, recipe or custom.
- A grudge between two families.
- An animal that matters for a reason.
- A door that should stay shut.

### Seeds (secret; Small; anchored to something already made)
- A relic your incarnation will find and not understand.
- A person who will recognise you by something you do not remember.
- A truth about how a famous thing really happened.
- A place that is waiting for you.
- A debt that belongs to you from before.

### Proverbs (said aloud; one sentence mortals say)
- "Never trust a tide that comes in twice."
- "Everyone owes the Auditor."
- (Write your own: what do ordinary people say about the world you made?)

## 7. Scribe: the Opening (read aloud)

> Before anything, there is a table and nothing on it.
> No light, no ground, no names. A space the size of a held breath.
> Then someone says a word, and the space has a shape.
> You have been here since before there were hours. Tonight you decide what comes next.

Then run the Prologue: God Sheets, then the First Word. Start the clock at the first Act.

|---|---|

| 0:30 | Round 1 Foundation: four Grand Acts (about 6 min each, plus deals) |

| 2:10 | Round 4 Weaving |

| 3:00 | Seal the Ledger; Incarnation (46 min) |

Times drift; the spare is about 24 minutes and goes to deals. For other group sizes use the table in [Running a Genesis Session](Running%20a%20Genesis%20Session.md).

**After the session:** photograph the Ledger and the Pacts; keep the Seeds private; ask Claude to turn the Ledger into a universe (see the end of the guide).
