# Running a Convocation

A Convocation is every cycle of [the Gameplay Loop](../concepts/The%20Gameplay%20Loop.md) after the first. The gods reconvene after a campaign, hear what their avatars did, settle their Pacts, welcome new gods, build more of the world, owe a new Doom for it, skip time if they like, hide new relics, and go down again. It takes about an hour and a half before Incarnation, so it fits as the first part of a session or as a short evening of its own. It is also the night the campaign's events merge into the universe's canon.

As everywhere in the loop: a base way, not the right way. Options are marked.

## Before the night
- **The Scribe brings** the sealed Ledger and the Entropy tally, the Pact Slips, the Relic Slips (which were found, which were not), the campaign's session log, the true Doom and what became of it.
- **Players bring** their avatar's sheet and their God Sheet.
- **Decide** who shapes the next Doom (Council or Keeper, as at Genesis) and whether to build one round (base) or two.

## Order of the night

| Step | What happens | Time |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Retirement | Each player ends their avatar | 10 min |
| 2. Reading of the Ledger | The Veil lifts for the gods; the Doom's outcome is written | 15 min |
| 3. Pacts due | Kept pacts pay out; broken ones cost | 10 min |
| 4. Arrivals | New gods, if any | 5 min each |
| 5. Building | One round of Acts (two as an option), with weights | 30 to 45 min |
| 6. Reckoning | Spend the new debt on the next Doom | 15 min |
| 7. The Turning | Optional time-skip | 5 min |
| 8. Relics | Each god hides a new relic | 5 min |
| 9. Incarnation | New avatars, now or next session | 25 min + 4 per god |

### 1. Retirement
Each player declares their avatar's end and writes a **Remnant** (one fact the life learned, carried into the next) and a **Legend** (one sentence mortals say about them). The four ends are in [the loop](../concepts/The%20Gameplay%20Loop.md#retirement): **Reborn** (the god returns later as a new avatar), **New chosen** (the avatar lives on as an NPC; the god picks another mortal), **Return** (the life closes; the god resumes), **Ascend** (the avatar joins the pantheon as a lesser god, and the player may play them as their god from now on). An avatar who died in play already ended as Reborn, unless the player ascends them now.

- *Option (keep playing):* a player may keep the same avatar through a Convocation if the Turning is none or a season. Their god still builds; the avatar learns nothing new.

### 2. Reading of the Ledger
The Scribe reads the campaign in brief from the session log: what the avatars did, which relics woke, how the Convergence went, or how the Doom landed. The gods hear it as gods, in character, and each may ask the Scribe one question about the world. Then the Scribe writes the Doom's outcome into the Ledger as an event: the transformation the Convergence made (a season, a scar, a seal, a cost, a new thing) or the ruin the landing left. If the Convergence's shape was left open, the Council decides it now.

### 3. Pacts due
Every Pact from the last cycle is read aloud.
- **Kept:** the god who kept it names one boon to the world, a Small act, free.
- **Broken:** the breaker's Fate worsens, or passes to their next avatar, and the wronged god gains a **Grievance**: their first Petition in the building round costs one Act instead of two.
- Either way the two gods may make a new Pact on the spot.

### 4. Arrivals
A new god can arrive at any Convocation: a new player, a player swapping gods, or an ascended avatar. Each newcomer says a First Word and takes one **Arrival Act** (Grand, 3 Entropy). A god whose player has moved on goes dormant: it stays in the pantheon, and the Scribe speaks for it if it must act.

### 5. Building
One round (base) or two (option). Each god takes one Act per round, with the same six steps as a Genesis Act (declare, speak the Word, anchor and mark, yes-and, the Note, the Tally), the same deals, and the same weights. Three kinds of Act:
- **Create**, as at Genesis: Grand 3, Middling 2, Small 1. Joint Acts count as Grand.
- **Intervene**: change something that exists. Bless, curse, raise, sink, split, crown, flood, answer a prayer. It costs the size of what is changed: a continent 3, a city or faction 2, a person or place 1.
- **Canonize**: a dead or ascended avatar becomes a saint, a legend, a constellation, a feast day. Free.

There is no Foundation round; anything goes from the first Act. Four gods building one round owe about 8; two rounds, about 16. That buys a Scar or a Blight after one round and a Scourge after two: shorter arcs than the first campaign unless the table builds big on purpose. Carried Entropy adds to it.

### 6. Reckoning
The new debt, plus anything carried, is spent on the next Doom from the same menu and by the same Council or Keeper as at Genesis. The public face is written and read aloud. Options, as in the loop: **Deferral** (carry Entropy forward on purpose; it grows by 1 each cycle it waits) and **Landing** (skip the next campaign and let the Doom come; the Scribe narrates it at its Scale and the world is changed; the debt is paid).

### 7. The Turning (optional)
The gods choose how much time passes before the next Incarnation. The Turning costs nothing: it is the world spending time, not the gods spending power. Whatever the choice, each god names one thing that happened in the gap, free, and the Scribe advances the calendar.

| Turning | How long | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| None | days | The world as the avatars left it. Avatars may continue. |
| A season | months | Wounds fresh, relics where they were left, the Legends still spreading. |
| A generation | 20 to 40 years | The avatars' children and students are grown. The gods name one faction that rose and one that fell. Each god says where their old relic went. Each god gives one new Proverb. |
| An age | a century or more | The avatars are myth. Each god rewrites one Proverb. Heirloom relics are lost or vaulted. The map may have changed: each god names one change, free. The calendar opens a new era. |

Past avatars get their `to:` dates. After an age, write an `era` note.

### 8. Relics
Each god hides a new relic for their next avatar, free, on a Relic Slip as at Genesis. Relics found last cycle become **heirlooms**: they keep their alone-power and lose their joining power, unless the god pays 1 Entropy to keep one in the next Convergence. Relics never found stay hidden; they still wake if found later.

### 9. Incarnation
Seal the Ledger and run Incarnation as at Genesis: concept, stats, Echo, Fate (from the new Pacts and the old consequences), Remnant, the Lead to the new relic, and the Veil. It can be done now or at the start of the next session.

## Options
- **Domain drift.** If an avatar's deeds leaned one way, the god may add an aspect to their Domain at step 1. The deity note's `domains` list grows.
- **Prayer.** Once per campaign a god may answer one prayer with a Small act in the world. It costs 1, added to the next debt. The Scribe plays it as a miracle.
- **Build only.** A table that wants to world-build without playing runs steps 4 to 8 and defers or lands the debt.
- **Two rounds.** A bigger build and a bigger Doom. Good when the table wants a long second campaign.

## Canon Merge night
A Convocation is when the campaign stops being local and becomes history.
1. Before the night, the Scribe runs `python3 _rules/tools/merge_instance.py . <instance id>` to draft the campaign's `canon: proposed` events as universe events, and resolves any Reality Rifts; see [Running and Logging a Campaign](Running%20and%20Logging%20a%20Campaign.md). If the universe is yours, accept them; if it is shared, open the canon-merge PR.
2. The Doom's outcome (step 2) is one more event.
3. The building round's creations are new notes, the Turning's gaps are events, an age is an `era`, retired avatars get `to:` dates, ascended ones get `deity` notes.
4. The next campaign is a new instance, forked at the new frontier. The [Note Wizard](../getting-started/The%20Note%20Wizard.md) writes any of these from a form.

## Scribe's checklist
- Before: run the merge; reread the Pacts and the Relic Slips; know what the Doom became; decide Council or Keeper and one round or two.
- Retirement first, so everyone knows who they are before the Veil lifts.
- Read the campaign in five minutes, not fifty. The gods were there.
- Keep the tally public; say the carried Entropy out loud before building starts.
- At the Reckoning, spend it all or defer it on purpose, never by accident.
- Seal the Ledger before Incarnation. Photograph it.
