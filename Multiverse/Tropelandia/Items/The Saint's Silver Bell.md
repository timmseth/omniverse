# The Saint's Silver Bell

*wondrous, rare (requires attunement).* A hand bell of tarnished silver that rings clear through fog.

**Effect.** Ring it as an action, once per day: as a ring of protection (+1 AC and saves) for 1 hour to everyone who heard it. Undead within 60 ft must succeed on a DC 13 Wisdom save or be turned for 1 minute.


The Sunken Chapel's altar bell.
