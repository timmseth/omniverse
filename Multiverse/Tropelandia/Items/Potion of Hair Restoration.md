# Potion of Hair Restoration

*potion, common.* A brown bottle labelled in Marlowe's handwriting: EXPERIMENTAL. DO NOT DRINK WHILE BALD.

**Effect.** Drink: regrow a full head of hair in 1 minute, in a colour of the DM's choice. Also works on the chin, the palms and, if spilled, the floor.
