# Ring of Opinions

*ring, uncommon (requires attunement).* A plain gold band that warms when it disagrees with you, which is often.

**Effect.** While worn, you have advantage on Insight checks and disadvantage on Persuasion checks, because the ring keeps interrupting.

**Curse.** Cannot be removed until you concede a point to it out loud, in company.

Returned to Glintfang by three separate heroes.
