# Anvil of Opinions

*wondrous, artifact.* A dwarven anvil of black iron, cold for three centuries, which speaks when struck and never agrees.

**Effect.** When struck, answers one question truthfully, then argues with the answer. Weapons forged upon it (by a smith of Karak-Dum-Dum's line) are +2 and refuse to be used for anything the Anvil considers beneath them.


The heart of Karak-Dum-Dum. The Reclamation Trust exists to light it again.
