# Marlowe's Pointed Hat

*wondrous, uncommon (requires attunement).* A tall blue hat with stars, bent at the tip, that fits anyone who puts it on.

**Effect.** While worn, people think you are funnier than you are: advantage on Performance checks to amuse.

**Curse.** Cannot be removed. People will not stop laughing at you, even when you are serious.

Marlowe's. He is not sure it was ever his.
