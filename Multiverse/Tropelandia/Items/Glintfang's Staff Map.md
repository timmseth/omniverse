# Glintfang's Staff Map

*wondrous, rare.* A kobold-drawn map of the Hoard, annotated in a very small hand with fire-exit routes and the location of the good biscuits.

**Effect.** Reveals the layout of Glintfang's Hoard. Advantage on Survival checks to navigate it.


Pip Ledgerclaw's own copy, lost in an audit.
