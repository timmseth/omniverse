# Wand of Mild Magic Missiles

*wand, uncommon.* A slim wand of birch, a little bent, with a label reading NOT FOR INDOOR USE.

**Effect.** 7 charges. Action: cast magic missile at 1st level (1 charge) or higher (1 charge per level). Each dart does 1d4+1 force and a small apology. Regains 1d6+1 charges at dawn; on a 1 for the last charge, the wand sulks and does nothing for a week.
