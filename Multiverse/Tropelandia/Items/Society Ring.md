# Society Ring

*ring, uncommon.* A plain iron ring with a tiny fish engraved inside the band.

**Effect.** Opens the doors of the Guildhall Below that are keyed to it, and marks the wearer to any Society member as one of their own, until the ring is reported stolen.


Belongs to a member who is not going to report it.
