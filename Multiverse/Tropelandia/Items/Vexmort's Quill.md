# Vexmort's Quill

*wondrous, very rare (requires attunement).* A raven's quill that never runs dry and writes in a hand that is not yours.

**Effect.** Any contract written with it is binding on all signatories, enforced by the Office of Eternal Records. The Office reads every contract.

**Curse.** Everything you write with it is filed. Everything.

Lord Vexmort's own.
