# Pot of Healing Soup

*consumable, common.* A dented copper pot, warm to the touch, of something with carrots in it.

**Effect.** Four servings. Eat a serving as an action: regain 1d8 hit points. The pot refills with cold soup at dawn, which heals nothing but is quite nice.
