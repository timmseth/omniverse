# Robe of Rent Receipts

*wondrous, uncommon (requires attunement).* A wizard's robe patched with what look like useful pockets and are in fact receipts.

**Effect.** As a robe of useful items, except every patch produces a rent receipt for a different property. Two patches, DM's choice, produce something useful anyway.


Marlowe's. Each receipt is unpaid.
