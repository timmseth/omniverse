# Cloak of Undeath Resistance

*wondrous, rare (requires attunement).* A grey clerk's cloak with ink-stains on the cuffs.

**Effect.** Resistance to necrotic damage; undead must succeed on a DC 13 Wisdom save to attack you, as they take you for staff.


Issued to Living Liaisons one through eleven. It outlasted them.
