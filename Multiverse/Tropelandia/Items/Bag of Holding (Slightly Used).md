# Bag of Holding (Slightly Used)

*wondrous, uncommon.* A worn leather satchel, heavier inside than out, with somebody's initials picked off the flap.

**Effect.** Holds 500 lb up to 64 cubic ft; always weighs 15 lb. The bag already contains 1d4 small items of a previous owner's, DM's choice.


Previous owner was a guest of Glintfang's and did not need it any more.
