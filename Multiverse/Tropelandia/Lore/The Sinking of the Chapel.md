# The Sinking of the Chapel

*Legend; half true.*

Father Mildred built his chapel on the firmest ground in the marsh, which is to say on a very large and patient frog. When the Church mortgaged the chapel to the Mildew Court, the frog, who had not been consulted, left. The chapel has been sinking ever since at the rate of one inch per unpaid year. The bell rings at midnight because Father Mildred is still trying to call the congregation back, and because he has a lot to say about the mortgage.
