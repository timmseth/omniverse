# The Unfiled One

*Prophecy; unknown true.*

When the backlog is cleared and the last form stamped, one shall come to the counter whose name is on no file: neither living nor dead, neither owed nor owing. The Office shall have nothing to give them, and must therefore give them everything. Vexmort has read this prophecy nine hundred times and has concluded that the safest course is never to clear the backlog.
