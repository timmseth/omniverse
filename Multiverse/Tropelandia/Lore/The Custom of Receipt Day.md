# The Custom of Receipt Day

*Custom; wholly true.*

Every Decembrr 30, every household in Last Receipt closes its books, calls its debts and gives its gifts, each with a receipt, so that nobody can later pretend a gift was a loan or a loan a gift. Glintfang's annual report is read in the square by a kobold on a box. It is always a good year for the dragon. The custom started as a joke about the dragon and became law when the dragon adopted it.
