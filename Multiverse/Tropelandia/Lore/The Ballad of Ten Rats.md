# The Ballad of Ten Rats

*Song; mostly true.*

A drinking song in eleven verses, one for each rat and one for the innkeeper. Each verse ends with the hero coming back up the stairs with an opinion instead of a rat. The last verse is about the spectacles. It is traditional for the whole tavern to shout 'TWO SILVER' at the end of every chorus, and for the innkeeper to pretend not to hear.
