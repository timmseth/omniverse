# The Founding Tavern

*Myth; mostly true.*

On the first morning of the first year, two doors opened on the same street: the Office of Eternal Records, and a tavern. The Office's first customer was a man who had died in the night. The tavern's first customer was the same man, who had decided to wait in comfort. The queue formed at the tavern and has never really left it. This is why every tavern in Tropelandia has a job notice, and why every job is the same job.
