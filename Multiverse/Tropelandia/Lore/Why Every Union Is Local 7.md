# Why Every Union Is Local 7

*Rumour; half true.*

There is no Local 1. There is no Local 6. Every union in Tropelandia, from the wizards to the goblins to the rats under the Gilded Tankard, is Local 7, and every one of them has the same tea urn. The story goes that the Grand Steward founded them all in one week and numbered them seven to make each sound established. The other story is that there were six before, and they are the reason the Office has a backlog.
