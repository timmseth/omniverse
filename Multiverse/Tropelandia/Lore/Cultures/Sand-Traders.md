# Sand-Traders

*Values:* water, a fair receipt, shade.

## Customs
- Every transaction is written, even a greeting.
- Water is never refused and never free.
- Guests of the dragon are spoken of in the present tense.

**Dress.** White linen, veils, a receipt book at the hip. **Food.** Dates, flatbread, lamb on holidays, water at every price.

**Names.** Zafira, Bashir, Thaddeus, Pip, Nadira, Omar; Dunewright, Two-Cups, Nib, Ledgerclaw, Saltmark.
