# Hold-Dwarves

*Values:* craft, memory, a settled account.

## Customs
- Every doorway bears a name; cross one out only with the family's consent.
- A grudge is entered in the book before bed, so it does not spoil.
- The first mug is poured for the Anvil.

**Dress.** Iron-grey wool, braids, tool-belts. **Food.** Ale, bread you could build with, stew.

**Names.** Brunhild, Hilde, Oona, Durgan, Thorvald, Agna; Ironbudget, Barrelchest, Grimblade, Pennywhistle, Stonewrit.
