# Marshfolk

*Values:* patience, courtesy to the dead, dry socks.

## Customs
- Ring the door-bell once when the fog comes in.
- Never step on a boardwalk plank with a name on it.
- Eel is served head-first to guests, tail-first to family.

**Dress.** Oilskins, wide hats, a bell at the belt. **Food.** Eel, pie, peat-smoked cheese.

**Names.** Prudence, Wendeline, Barnaby, Rosalind, Mildred, Gribble, Tess; Marsh-Tooth, Dimly, Stoutwhistle, Quickblade, Pettigrew, Underbough.
