# Woodfolk

*Values:* hospitality, listening, a locked door at night.

## Customs
- Knock twice on any inn door and wait; a real inn answers once.
- Leave a pot of honey for the trees in Sodden.
- Never review a place you have not left.

**Dress.** Green and brown, hoods, a honey-pot charm. **Food.** Venison, honey-cakes, mead.

**Names.** Tobias, Marigold, Fennimore, Grizzle, Hazel, Rowan; Ashgrove, Thorne, Honeywell, Oakhollow, Bramblecote.
