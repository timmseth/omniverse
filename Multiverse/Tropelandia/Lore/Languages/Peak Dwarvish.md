# Peak Dwarvish

*Spoken by dwarves of the Crooked Peaks, the Anvil.* Low, slow and heavily consonanted; every noun has a grudge-case.

| Say | Means |
|---|---|
| Khazâd-dûm-dum | the hold, said with longing |
| Grum | a grudge, minor |
| Grummak | a grudge, notarised |
| Baruk! | cheers, or charge |
