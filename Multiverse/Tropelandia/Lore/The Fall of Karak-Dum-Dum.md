# The Fall of Karak-Dum-Dum

*History; wholly true.*

In the year 1189 the miners of Karak-Dum-Dum broke into a cavern that was already lit. What they met there asked them a question. The records say they dug too deep; the journal says they were invited, and that they went politely, as dwarves do, and that the hold emptied in a week not from terror but from curiosity. The Anvil went cold the day the last smith went down to ask what the question was. The Trust has been trying to reclaim the halls for three centuries. Nobody has asked the question.
