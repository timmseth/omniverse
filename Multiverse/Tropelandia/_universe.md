# Tropelandia

A Munchkin-style parody of every fantasy and RPG trope you have ever rolled your eyes at, played with a straight face. Towers are rented, chosen ones have paperwork, and the dark lord is mostly worried about his quarterly numbers.

Players can do whatever they like. The world will have a funny, workable answer.
