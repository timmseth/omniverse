# The Guildhall Below

Beneath the Port Pilferage fish market sits the Guildhall of the Honourable Society of Independent Acquisitionists, a thieves' guild with a dress code, a membership fee and a vault that has never, officially, been robbed. The party's job is either to join, rob or inspect it. The guild would rather they chose joining.

## Hooks
- Rob the guild's vault.
- Join the guild (and survive initiation).
- Retrieve something stolen from a client.

## Rumours
- Everyone in Port Pilferage is a member, or owes one.
- The Guildmaster's parlour has a back exit that is also a front exit.
- The vault door is a riddle, and so is the key.

## Wandering encounters (d6)
- 1: Two thieves argue about commission
- 2: A pickpocket attempts the party (DC 13 Perception)
- 3: A guard asks to see membership cards
- 4: A forger offers a good price on 'certificates'
- 5: The lights flicker; someone has pawned the generator
- 6: A cat that is probably a spy watches from a ledge
