# The Goblin Union Hall

A hollow oak in Whisperwood, the headquarters of Local 7 of the United Brotherhood of Goblins, Hobs and Allied Skulkers. They are on strike. The picket line has not moved in six weeks, and neither has the management. They would very much like someone to mediate, and are prepared to attack anyone who is not them.

## Hooks
- Mediate the strike.
- Cross the picket line.
- Find out what the goblins actually want.

## Rumours
- The Union has a strike fund and a grudge.
- The Hobgoblin Foreman has not slept in six days.
- The Boss is a very short man in a very long coat.

## Wandering encounters (d6)
- 1: Pickets chanting: 'WHAT DO WE WANT? BREAK ROOM!'
- 2: A goblin hands the party a flyer
- 3: A scab (a squirrel) scurries through
- 4: 3 angry goblins (hp 7, AC 15)
- 5: A tired hobgoblin asks for a mediator
- 6: A long, uneasy silence; someone coughs
