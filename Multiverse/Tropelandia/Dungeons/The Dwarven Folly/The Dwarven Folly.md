# The Dwarven Folly

Karak-Dum-Dum was the proudest dwarven hold in the Crooked Peaks, right up to the day its miners dug a little too deep. The upper halls are still impressive, the forge still sings, and something below has been politely knocking for three hundred years.

## Hooks
- Reclaim the lost halls for the surviving clan.
- Find out what the dwarves dug into.
- Recover the Anvil of Opinions, a legendary forge.

## Rumours
- The forge sings at midnight; the song has a second voice.
- Every miner who went down came back with a cough and a secret.
- The dwarves left their beer. A dwarf does not leave beer.

## Wandering encounters (d6)
- 1: Distant hammering, three beats, stop
- 2: 1d4 duergar scouts, arguing about a map
- 3: A cold wind from below, smelling of old stone and something wrong
- 4: A cave-in (DC 13 Dex or 2d6)
- 5: A lost miner's ghost asks for a hat
- 6: Silence, which in a dwarven hall is itself a warning
