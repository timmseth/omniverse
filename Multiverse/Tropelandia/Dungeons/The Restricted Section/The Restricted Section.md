# The Restricted Section

The Restricted Section of the Thaumaturgic Academy at Scholars' Spire holds every book that was too dangerous to burn, too embarrassing to lose and too interesting to ignore. The books bite, the shelves rearrange and the Librarian has seen every excuse. A student's overdue book is the party's excuse to go inside.

## Hooks
- Retrieve an overdue book.
- Find a spell the Dean forgot.
- Investigate the student who vanished into the stacks.

## Rumours
- The books are alive and a bit hungry.
- The Librarian is older than the Academy.
- Nobody's ever finished the Forbidden Index.

## Wandering encounters (d6)
- 1: A book flaps past, snapping
- 2: Shelves rearrange; the path home changes
- 3: A student ghost asks for a reference
- 4: Whispers from a banned poetry book
- 5: A scroll unrolls and tries to read you
- 6: A distant, furious SHHH
