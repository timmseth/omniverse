# The Sunken Chapel of Mild Dread

A small chapel that sank into the Gently Haunted Marsh centuries ago and refuses to finish the job. Its bell still rings at midnight, its dead are polite but persistent, and its altar is wanted by three different cults, none of whom has remembered to bring a boat.

## Hooks
- Recover the chapel's bell for the village of Barrowdale.
- Escort a priest who wants to bless the dead.
- Find the relic three cults are fighting over.

## Rumours
- The bell rings by itself at midnight; one note is flat and the dead are very sensitive about it.
- The priest vanished here and sends angry letters from the crypt.
- A reliquary glows under the water; touching it makes you sneeze.

## Wandering encounters (d6)
- 1: The bell rings; all undead in the chapel wake up
- 2: 1d4 skeletons shuffle past, nodding politely
- 3: A cold spot; a ghostly voice asks directions
- 4: A swarm of marsh bats (hp 5, AC 12)
- 5: Water rises 1 ft; disadvantage on Dex
- 6: A distant hymn, slightly off-key
