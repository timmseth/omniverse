# The Lich's Filing Office

In the Hollow Deep, beneath the world, sits the Office of Eternal Records: a vast bureaucracy run by Lord Vexmort the Undying, who found immortality easy and paperwork impossible. Every soul he has ever bound is filed here, in triplicate. Heroes come to destroy him. His secretary has asked that they take a number.

## Hooks
- Destroy the lich's phylactery.
- Retrieve a soul from the files.
- File a complaint about a particular death.

## Rumours
- The lich has not left his desk in 900 years.
- The phylactery is somewhere in the filing system.
- The waiting room has no exit.

## Wandering encounters (d6)
- 1: A skeletal clerk hands the party a form
- 2: A queue forms behind the party
- 3: A bell rings; a 15-minute coffee break is declared
- 4: A ghostly auditor arrives
- 5: A stamp falls from the ceiling (2d6)
- 6: A distant voice: 'WHO LOST THE FILE?'
