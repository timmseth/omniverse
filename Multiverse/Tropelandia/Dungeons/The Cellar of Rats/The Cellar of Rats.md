# The Cellar of Rats

The cellar of the Gilded Tankard inn, Barrowdale's most reliable source of rodents. The innkeeper has posted the same job notice for nine years: ten rats, two silver pieces. The rats have noticed the notice, and have views.

## Hooks
- Take the innkeeper's job and clear out the rats.
- Find out why the rats are so organised.
- Recover the stock the innkeeper swears he never lost.

## Rumours
- The rats have been seen carrying tiny notices.
- Nobody who went down came back with ten rats; they came back with an opinion.
- The innkeeper has not been down there in nine years, and keeps changing the subject.

## Wandering encounters (d6)
- 1: 1d4 rats scurry past carrying a tiny crate
- 2: A distant squeak, followed by a very small cheer
- 3: A rat pauses, reads the party's boots, and leaves
- 4: 2 giant rats, hungry (hp 7, AC 12)
- 5: The innkeeper's boy, sent to check on the party, bearing cheese
- 6: Nothing; the dark seems smug
