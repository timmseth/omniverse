# RAT-01 Cellar Stairs

> Twelve stone steps drop into a cold that smells of old wine, wet stone and something furry. A lantern hangs from a hook, its flame trembling at every squeak from below. The step at the bottom is worn into a hollow, as though a great many small feet have been using it.

## Exits
- **south** (archway, open): [[RAT-02 Barrel Hall|barrel hall]]
- **up** (stairs, open): outside — Up to the Gilded Tankard's kitchen

## Features
- A hook with a spare lantern
- A hand-lettered sign: DANGER (RATS) (ALSO STAIRS)
- Chalk tally marks on the wall, nine years of them

## DM notes
Quiet start. The squeaking is audible here; make the party feel watched, not attacked.
