# Marlowe's Tower

A narrow tower of grey stone that appeared overnight during the Pie Contest Incident and has been 'temporarily' on the Mayor's judging table ever since. Marlowe the Mildly Menacing lives at the top, three months behind on rent, and defends it with a staircase, a rule about footwear, and a lot of glassware.

## Hooks
- Burgle the tower while Marlowe is out.
- Apprentice yourself to Marlowe (and learn his rent troubles).
- Deliver a legal notice to the top floor.

## Rumours
- The tower is smaller on the outside than inside, and the Mayor will not discuss this.
- Marlowe keeps a rival's secret recipe in his study.
- Nobody has seen him leave in a month; someone has seen the milk delivery though.

## Wandering encounters (d6)
- 1: A sheet of paper flutters down the stairwell: an eviction notice
- 2: A floating broom drifts by, sweeping with great resentment
- 3: The stairs shuffle themselves (re-roll direction)
- 4: A flying inkpot attacks, then apologises
- 5: A magical rodent scuttles past, carrying a note
- 6: Distant shouting: 'WHO PUT THE ROOF ON THE JUDGES' TABLE?'
