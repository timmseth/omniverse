# Glintfang's Hoard

Glintfang is a young gold dragon with a head for business, a hoard she calls a portfolio and a long list of visiting heroes she has politely turned into guests. Her lair is a sandstone cathedral of traps, appraisals and exit interviews. She would rather trade than fight, but her definition of a fair trade is generous to Glintfang.

## Hooks
- Rob the hoard.
- Trade with the dragon for a favour.
- Rescue a merchant who got stuck in an exit interview.

## Rumours
- She appraises everything, including you.
- The hoard moves; she rearranges it daily.
- Nobody leaves without signing something.

## Wandering encounters (d6)
- 1: A sandstorm sweeps the dune mouth
- 2: A kobold intern with a clipboard
- 3: A visitor in a tailored robe, who is Glintfang in disguise
- 4: A magic mirror asks the party's net worth
- 5: An appraiser's apprentice offers a quote
- 6: Distant gold coins rattling, as if the dragon is sleeping
