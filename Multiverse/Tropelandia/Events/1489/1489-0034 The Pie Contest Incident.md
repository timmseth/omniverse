# The Pie Contest Incident

**4 Fogruary 1489, Barrowdale.**

Marlowe's entry, "Pie of Mild Menace", beat Dame Rosalind's three-time-champion blackberry. Accusations of sabotage followed. Nobody is sure how a tower ended up in the middle of the judges' table.

[[Mayor Bartholomew Pettigrew]] chaired the judging and has declined to comment on the tower.

Aftermath: [[Marlowe the Mildly Menacing]] and [[Dame Rosalind Quickblade]] are now rivals.
