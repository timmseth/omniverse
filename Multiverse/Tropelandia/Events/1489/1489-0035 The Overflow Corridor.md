# The Overflow Corridor

*Year 1489, day 35.*

A clerk reports that Overflow Corridor 9 does not lead to the lower archive but to a cold hall full of foreign dead, queueing in good order. Lord Vexmort reads the report, files it under PENDING, and assigns a clerk to the counter. The clerk has been there two centuries in the other world's time, which the Office considers a rounding error.

This is the Tropelandia end of the Reality Rift to [[Multiverse/Ashfall/Dungeons/The Cold Vault/The Cold Vault|The Cold Vault]] in Ashfall. A party crossing in either direction is a rift event in both universes and needs both maintainers to agree before it is canon.
