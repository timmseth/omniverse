# Marlowe's Rented Tower

An instance of [[Wizard in a Tower]], played for laughs. The tower sits on [[The Crooked Lane]] and belongs to the landlord, [[Mayor Bartholomew Pettigrew]].

## Hooks
- **Apprentice:** Marlowe needs someone to carry his books, defend his honour and, ideally, cover a month of rent.
- **Deliver a package:** a very small parcel has been sitting at the bottom of the stairs for weeks.
- **Help with the landlord:** the Mayor has stopped saying hello.
