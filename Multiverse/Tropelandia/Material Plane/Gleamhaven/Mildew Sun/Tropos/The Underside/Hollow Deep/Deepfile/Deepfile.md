# Deepfile

> Deepfile is a town of the dead who have not been allowed to leave. Lanterns of cold fire line streets paved with filing-slate. Skeletons in ink-stained cuffs hurry past with armfuls of scrolls. Everyone is polite, nobody is in a hurry, and the queue at the Office has not moved since the founding.

The town that grew up around the Office of Eternal Records, where the dead of Tropelandia go to be processed and, in the absence of anyone approving the processing, stay. It is the quietest town in the world and the one with the longest waiting list.
