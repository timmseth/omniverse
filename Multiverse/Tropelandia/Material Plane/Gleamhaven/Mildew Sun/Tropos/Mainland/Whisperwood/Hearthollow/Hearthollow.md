# Hearthollow

> Hearthollow is a town of timber and honey-smoke at the edge of Whisperwood, built around a hollow oak so large the market fits inside it. The trees at the forest's edge lean in to listen. The innkeepers lean in to smile.

A logging and honey town that has prospered by being the last comfortable place before the deep forest. It has learned not to ask which of its inns are inns.
