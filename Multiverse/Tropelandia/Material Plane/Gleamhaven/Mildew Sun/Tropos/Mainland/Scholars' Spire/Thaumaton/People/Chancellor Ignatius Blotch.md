# Chancellor Ignatius Blotch

*Chancellor of the Thaumaturgic Academy.* Usually found at [[Thaumaturgic Academy Gate]] in [[Thaumaton]].

**Voice.** Grand, vague, speaks in subordinate clauses that never close.

**Wants** the Restricted Section to stay restricted. **Fears** the Dean; the Librarian; the books.

**Secret.** The Chancellor has been a projected illusion for eleven months. The real one is in the Restricted Section, reading, and does not want to be disturbed.
