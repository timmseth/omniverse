# Thaumaton

> Thaumaton is a city of towers that do not agree on which way is up. Spells fizz off the rooftops like fireworks, scholars argue in the streets in three dead languages, and every doorway has a plaque listing who is not allowed through it.

The seat of the Thaumaturgic Academy, the Licensing Board's head office, and the Union's regional branch, which means the city has more rules per head than any other place in Tropelandia and follows fewer of them.
