# Grudgehold

> Grudgehold is carved into the face of the Crooked Peaks in tiers, each tier older and angrier than the one below. The air smells of forge smoke and resentment. Every doorway has a name chiselled over it, and most of the names have been crossed out.

The dwarves who fled Karak-Dum-Dum built Grudgehold a day's march away, close enough to look at the old hold every morning. They have done this for three hundred years. They intend to keep doing it.
