# Marlowe the Mildly Menacing

A reclusive archmage who is reclusive mainly because he cannot afford visitors. His fireballs are excellent and his credit rating is not.

His tower is [[Marlowe's Tower]], a rented flat on [[The Crooked Lane]]. He is a member of [[Wizards Union Local 7]].

His rivalry with [[Dame Rosalind Quickblade]] began at [[1489-0034 The Pie Contest Incident]].
