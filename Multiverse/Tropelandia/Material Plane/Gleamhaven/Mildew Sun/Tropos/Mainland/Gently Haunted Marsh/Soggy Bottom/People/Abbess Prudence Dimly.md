# Abbess Prudence Dimly

*Abbess of the Shrine of the Damp Lady.* Usually found at [[Shrine of the Damp Lady]] in [[Soggy Bottom]].

**Voice.** Slow, kind, and absolutely certain. Never raises her voice; lowers it instead.

**Wants** the chapel's dead at rest, in writing. **Fears** that Father Mildred was right about everything.

**Secret.** She knows exactly why the chapel sank: her predecessor mortgaged it to the Mildew Court, and the marsh foreclosed.
