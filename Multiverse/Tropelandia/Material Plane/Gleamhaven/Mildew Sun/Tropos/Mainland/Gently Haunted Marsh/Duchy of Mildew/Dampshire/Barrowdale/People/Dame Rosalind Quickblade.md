# Dame Rosalind Quickblade

A three-time champion of the Barrowdale pie contest, and the only knight in the Duchy who carries a rolling pin as a backup weapon. She has never lost a duel and has lost exactly one pie contest.

Her rivalry with [[Marlowe the Mildly Menacing]] began at [[1489-0034 The Pie Contest Incident]].
