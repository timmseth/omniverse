# Soggy Bottom

> Soggy Bottom sits on stilts above a marsh that has never quite decided whether it is land. Boardwalks creak, eels are sold by the yard, and every house has a bell by the door to ring when the fog gets opinions.

Founded by a congregation that followed a sinking chapel and stopped when it did. The village is devout, damp and deeply unbothered by its dead, who are considered rate-paying residents.
