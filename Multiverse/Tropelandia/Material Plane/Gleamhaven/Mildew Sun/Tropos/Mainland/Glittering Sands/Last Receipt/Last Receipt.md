# Last Receipt

> Last Receipt is a ring of white-washed houses around a well, every door painted with a price list. Awnings crackle in the hot wind. Beyond the last house the dunes glitter with gold dust, and somewhere out there a dragon is doing her quarterly accounts.

The last stop before the dunes and the first place caravans count their money afterwards. The town has made a living from both ends of the journey, and from the dragon in between.
