# Vashti Nine-Fingers

*Guildmaster of the Honourable Society.* Usually found at [[Nine-Fingers Pawn and Loan]] in [[Greyharbour]].

**Voice.** Low, amused, never hurried. Calls a theft 'an acquisition' and a murder 'a regrettable acquisition'.

**Wants** the Society's ledger back. **Fears** the Court learning who is in the ledger.

**Secret.** The tenth finger is in a box in the Guildhall. It is the Society's seal, and it still signs things.
