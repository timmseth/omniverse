# Greyharbour

> Greyharbour is grey: grey stone quays, grey rain, grey gulls with grey intentions. The fish market shouts, the rigging creaks, and in every doorway someone is politely not watching you. The city smells of salt, tar and a transaction in progress.

The largest port on the Sullen Coast and the headquarters of its most respectable thieves. Greyharbour runs on an understanding: the Society steals from visitors, the visitors buy it back, and the Harbourmaster takes a modest percentage of the gratitude.
