# The Stag

*Lord of the Open Door.* Domains: forests, hospitality, the hunt, laughter. Symbol: A laughing stag's head.

## Tenets
- A guest is sacred; a host is sacred; the bill is between them.
- Laugh at what is trying to eat you.
- Leave the door open and the fire lit.

- woodfolk
- innkeepers, the real ones
- goblins, grudgingly

**Boons.** a warm bed. **Wrath.** every inn is full, and the one that is not is a mimic.
