# Grudge Night

*Octoberish 13.*

## Customs
- Every grudge in the book is read aloud, oldest first; it takes all night.
- A new grudge may be entered only if an old one is settled.
- The Anvil is toasted, and the toast is answered from the mountain.

## Hooks
- Someone has entered a grudge against the party. They do not know who.
