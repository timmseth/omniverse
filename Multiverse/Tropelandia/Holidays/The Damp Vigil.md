# The Damp Vigil

*Fogruary 1.*

## Customs
- Candles are floated on the marsh for the polite dead.
- Bells are rung once at midnight; twice is an emergency.
- Eel is eaten in silence.

## Hooks
- The Sunken Chapel's bell always answers. This year it answered first.
