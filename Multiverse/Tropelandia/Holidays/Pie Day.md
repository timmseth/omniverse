# Pie Day

*Mayhap 15.*

## Customs
- The Mayor judges the pie contest.
- Rivalries are declared, renewed or settled by pie.
- Any tower that appears on the judging table is, by tradition, disqualified.

## Hooks
- Marlowe's tower is still on the table. The contest is in a week.
