# The Arc: three sessions, levels 1 to 3

## Session 1: Ten Rats (level 1)
Quest: [[Ten Rats]]. Dungeon: [[The Cellar of Rats]].
Barnaby offers the job. The party goes down. The rats have a king, a position and the innkeeper's spectacles. End the session at the Mayor's ruling, or with the rats' tiny notice nailed to the Tankard's door. Level up to 2.

Threads to plant: Fizzwick's Union card gets him a nod from the Rat Sommelier. The Mayor mentions the auditor and changes the subject. Grizzle's pamphlet is on the bar.

## Session 2: The Strike (level 2)
Quest: [[The Strike]]. Dungeon: [[The Goblin Union Hall]].
The Reeve's letter reaches Barrowdale: end the strike, either way. The road to Hearthollow passes a very nice inn (do not go in yet; let Wren notice the towels). The Union Hall, the Chief, the hat with the Court's seal. End with terms, or with the party holding the strike fund and a difficult choice. Level up to 3.

Threads to plant: the Court's informants. The Roadside Welcome's invitation. Grand Steward Grimsbane's name on the goblins' charter.

## Session 3: The Rent Is Due (level 3)
Quest: [[The Rent Is Due]]. Dungeon: [[Marlowe's Tower]].
The Pie Contest is in three days and the tower is still on the table. The Union, the Mayor and Rosalind each hire the party. Up the tower: the broom, the slime, the study, the receipts made out *to* the tower. The contest, with the Duke's auditor judging, is the finale: who wins, who is evicted, and what the tower wants.

Where it goes next: [[Ring Once]] (the Abbess has heard of the party), [[Honest Reviews]] (Wren's aunt), or [[The Ledger]] (the Harbourmaster has a job and a boat). The campaign is open from here; propose canon merges for anything the table changed.
