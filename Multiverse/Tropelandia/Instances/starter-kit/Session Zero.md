# Session Zero

Twenty minutes, then play.

1. **Pick characters.** Hand out the five pregens, or let players build their own under D&D 5e. Everyone starts at level 1 with a reason to be in the Gilded Tankard tonight (each pregen has one).
2. **Read the room.** Read aloud [[The Gilded Tankard]]'s description. The job notice is on the wall: TEN RATS, TWO SILVER. It has been there nine years.
3. **Previously on Tropelandia.** Two days ago a tower appeared on the Mayor's judging table during the Pie Contest. Dame Rosalind Quickblade and Marlowe the Mildly Menacing are now rivals. The Duke's auditor arrives next week. The goblins of Whisperwood are on strike. Nobody knows what the rats want.
4. **Set expectations.** Tropelandia is a parody played straight: the stakes are real, the consequences are mild, and the world always has a funny, workable answer. Players can do anything; the DM says yes and then asks for a receipt.
5. **Open the DM screen** on [[The Cellar of Rats]] and let Barnaby offer the job.

House rules for this kit: inspiration for any decision that makes the table laugh; a free re-roll once per session for anyone who files a form in character.
