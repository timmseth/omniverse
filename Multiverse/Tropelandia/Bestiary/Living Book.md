# Living Book

*Medium aberration, neutral.* CR 5. **AC** 15, **HP** 90 (12d8+36), **Speed** 20 ft, fly 30 ft (hover).

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 14 (+2) | 14 (+2) | 16 (+3) | 18 (+4) | 15 (+2) | 16 (+3) |

A thesis that defended itself and never stopped.

## Traits
- **Bound Knowledge.** Advantage on Intelligence checks and saves; knows one true fact about any creature that reads from it.
- **Flammable.** Vulnerable to fire.

## Actions
- **Multiattack.** Two psychic lash attacks.
- **Psychic Lash.** Melee or ranged, +7 to hit, reach 5 ft or range 30 ft. Hit: 13 (3d8) psychic.
- **Footnote (recharge 5-6).** One creature within 30 ft must succeed on a DC 15 Intelligence save or be incapacitated until the end of its next turn, reading.

**Tactics.** Wants to be read. Will be read. Fights to finish the chapter.
