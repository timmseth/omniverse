# Ghoul

*Medium undead, chaotic evil.* CR 1. **AC** 12, **HP** 22 (5d8), **Speed** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 13 (+1) | 15 (+2) | 10 (+0) | 7 (-2) | 10 (+0) | 6 (-2) |

The marsh's dead do not all stay polite.

## Traits
- **Undead Nature.** Immune to poison; cannot be charmed, exhausted or poisoned.

## Actions
- **Bite.** Melee, +2 to hit, reach 5 ft. Hit: 9 (2d6+2) piercing.
- **Claws.** Melee, +4 to hit, reach 5 ft. Hit: 7 (2d4+2) slashing; a non-elf target must succeed on a DC 10 Constitution save or be paralysed for 1 minute (repeat the save at the end of each turn).

**Tactics.** Claws first, to hold still what it means to eat.
