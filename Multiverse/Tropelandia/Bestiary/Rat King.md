# Rat King

*Small beast, lawful neutral.* CR 0.5. **AC** 12, **HP** 22 (5d6+5), **Speed** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 9 (-1) | 15 (+2) | 13 (+1) | 8 (-1) | 12 (+1) | 12 (+1) |

Nine years of unpaid bills, a tiny crown, and a legal position that is, annoyingly, quite strong.

## Traits
- **Keen Smell.** As giant rat.
- **Cunning.** Advantage on Insight and Deception checks; knows the terms of every contract posted in the Gilded Tankard.

## Actions
- **Bite.** Melee, +4 to hit, reach 5 ft. Hit: 5 (1d6+2) piercing.
- **Royal Decree.** Each rat within 30 ft that can hear the King gains advantage on its next attack roll.

**Tactics.** Negotiates from the throne. Fights only if the throne is threatened.
