# Wraith

*Medium undead, neutral evil.* CR 5. **AC** 13, **HP** 67 (9d8+27), **Speed** 0 ft, fly 60 ft (hover).

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 6 (-2) | 16 (+3) | 16 (+3) | 12 (+1) | 14 (+2) | 15 (+2) |

Department heads of the Office of Eternal Records.

## Traits
- **Incorporeal Movement.** As specter.
- **Sunlight Sensitivity.** As specter.
- **Resistances.** Acid, cold, fire, lightning, thunder; non-magical weapons. Immune to necrotic and poison.

## Actions
- **Life Drain.** Melee, +6 to hit, reach 5 ft. Hit: 21 (4d8+3) necrotic; DC 14 Constitution save or the target's hit point maximum is reduced by the damage taken until it finishes a long rest.
- **Create Specter.** Targets a humanoid that died violently within the last minute; its spirit rises as a specter under the wraith's control.

**Tactics.** Senior management of the dead. Delegates the biting to specters.
