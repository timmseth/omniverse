# Ghost Judge

*Medium undead, lawful neutral.* CR 4. **AC** 11, **HP** 45 (10d8), **Speed** 0 ft, fly 40 ft (hover).

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 7 (-2) | 13 (+1) | 10 (+0) | 14 (+2) | 15 (+2) | 17 (+3) |

Three of them sit in the Office's Court of Appeals. The backlog is eternal.

## Traits
- **Ethereal Sight.** As ghost.
- **Incorporeal Movement.** As ghost.
- **Due Process.** Cannot attack a creature that has an appeal pending.

## Actions
- **Withering Touch.** As ghost.
- **Horrifying Visage.** As ghost.
- **Verdict.** One creature within 30 ft must succeed on a DC 13 Charisma save or be restrained by a sense of guilt until the end of its next turn.

**Tactics.** Hears the case. Rules against. Appeals are heard by the next judge.
