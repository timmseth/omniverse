# Specter

*Medium undead, chaotic evil.* CR 1. **AC** 12, **HP** 22 (5d8), **Speed** 0 ft, fly 50 ft (hover).

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (-5) | 14 (+2) | 11 (+0) | 10 (+0) | 10 (+0) | 11 (+0) |

Clerks, students and apprentices who died with work unfinished.

## Traits
- **Incorporeal Movement.** Can move through creatures and objects as difficult terrain; 5 (1d10) force damage if it ends its turn inside an object.
- **Sunlight Sensitivity.** Disadvantage on attacks and Perception checks relying on sight in sunlight.

## Actions
- **Life Drain.** Melee, +4 to hit, reach 5 ft. Hit: 10 (3d6) necrotic; DC 10 Constitution save or the target's hit point maximum is reduced by the damage taken until it finishes a long rest.

**Tactics.** Drifts through the wall, drains, drifts back.
