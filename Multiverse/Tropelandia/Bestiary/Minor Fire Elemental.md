# Minor Fire Elemental

*Medium elemental, neutral.* CR 2. **AC** 13, **HP** 20 (3d8+6), **Speed** 50 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10 (+0) | 17 (+3) | 14 (+2) | 6 (-2) | 10 (+0) | 7 (-2) |

Sparks from the Anvil that got ideas.

## Traits
- **Fire Form.** Can move through a 1-inch gap; a creature that touches it or hits it in melee within 5 ft takes 5 (1d10) fire.
- **Illumination.** Bright light 30 ft, dim 30 ft more.
- **Water Susceptibility.** Takes 1 cold damage per 5 ft moved through water or per gallon splashed.
- **Immunities.** Fire, poison; resistant to non-magical weapons.

## Actions
- **Touch.** Melee, +4 to hit, reach 5 ft. Hit: 7 (2d6) fire; a flammable target ignites and takes 5 (1d10) fire at the start of each of its turns until it uses an action to put the fire out.

**Tactics.** Runs along anything flammable, which in a dwarven brewery is everything.
