# Hobgoblin Shop Steward

*Medium humanoid (goblinoid), lawful neutral.* CR 0.5. **AC** 18, **HP** 11 (2d8+2), **Speed** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 13 (+1) | 12 (+1) | 12 (+1) | 12 (+1) | 10 (+0) | 13 (+1) |

Elected, armoured, and briefed.

## Traits
- **Martial Advantage.** Once per turn, +7 (2d6) damage to a creature if an ally is within 5 ft of it.

## Actions
- **Longsword.** Melee, +3 to hit, reach 5 ft. Hit: 5 (1d8+1) slashing, or 6 (1d10+1) two-handed.
- **Point of Order.** Each goblin within 30 ft that can hear the steward gains 5 temporary hit points.

**Tactics.** Holds the line and the agenda. Will negotiate mid-combat if offered terms in writing.
