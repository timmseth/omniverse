# Mage

*Medium humanoid (any), any.* CR 6. **AC** 12, **HP** 40 (9d8), **Speed** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 9 (-1) | 14 (+2) | 11 (+0) | 17 (+3) | 12 (+1) | 11 (+0) |

The baseline for every wizard, librarian and dragon-in-disguise in Tropelandia.

## Traits
- **Spellcasting (DC 14, +6).** Cantrips: fire bolt, light, mage hand, prestidigitation. 1st (4): detect magic, mage armor, magic missile, shield. 2nd (3): misty step, suggestion. 3rd (3): counterspell, fireball, fly. 4th (3): greater invisibility, ice storm. 5th (1): cone of cold.

## Actions
- **Dagger.** Melee or ranged, +5 to hit, reach 5 ft or range 20/60 ft. Hit: 4 (1d4+2) piercing.
- **Fire Bolt.** Ranged spell, +6 to hit, range 120 ft. Hit: 11 (2d10) fire.

**Tactics.** Shield when hit, misty step when cornered, fireball when the furniture is not theirs.
