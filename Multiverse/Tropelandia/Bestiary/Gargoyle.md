# Gargoyle

*Medium elemental, chaotic evil.* CR 2. **AC** 15, **HP** 52 (7d8+21), **Speed** 30 ft, fly 60 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 15 (+2) | 11 (+0) | 16 (+3) | 6 (-2) | 11 (+0) | 7 (-2) |

Every chapel and academy in Tropelandia has them, and they are all listening.

## Traits
- **False Appearance.** Indistinguishable from an inanimate statue while motionless.
- **Resistances.** Non-magical weapons that are not adamantine; immune to poison; cannot be exhausted, petrified or poisoned.

## Actions
- **Multiattack.** One bite and one claws attack.
- **Bite.** Melee, +4 to hit, reach 5 ft. Hit: 5 (1d6+2) piercing.
- **Claws.** Melee, +4 to hit, reach 5 ft. Hit: 5 (1d6+2) slashing.

**Tactics.** Waits to be walked past. Then does not wait.
