# Flying Book

*Tiny construct, unaligned.* CR 0.125. **AC** 14, **HP** 5 (2d4), **Speed** 0 ft, fly 40 ft (hover).

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4 (-3) | 16 (+3) | 10 (+0) | 8 (-1) | 10 (+0) | 6 (-2) |

Overdue, and resentful.

## Traits
- **Flock.** Advantage on attacks if another flying book is within 5 ft of the target.
- **False Appearance.** Indistinguishable from a shelved book while motionless.

## Actions
- **Paper Cut.** Melee, +4 to hit, reach 5 ft. Hit: 3 (1d4+1) slashing.
- **Recite.** One creature within 10 ft must succeed on a DC 10 Wisdom save or have disadvantage on its next attack roll while it loses its place.

**Tactics.** Swarm the reader. Flee a lit candle.
