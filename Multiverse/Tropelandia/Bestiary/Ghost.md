# Ghost

*Medium undead, any.* CR 4. **AC** 11, **HP** 45 (10d8), **Speed** 0 ft, fly 40 ft (hover).

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 7 (-2) | 13 (+1) | 10 (+0) | 10 (+0) | 12 (+1) | 17 (+3) |

Death in Tropelandia is a change of address.

## Traits
- **Ethereal Sight.** Can see 60 ft into the Ethereal Plane.
- **Incorporeal Movement.** Can move through creatures and objects as difficult terrain; takes 5 (1d10) force damage if it ends its turn inside an object.

## Actions
- **Withering Touch.** Melee, +5 to hit, reach 5 ft. Hit: 17 (4d6) necrotic.
- **Horrifying Visage.** Each non-undead creature within 60 ft that can see it must succeed on a DC 13 Wisdom save or be frightened for 1 minute; on a failure by 5 or more the target also ages 1d4 x 10 years.
- **Possession (recharge 6).** One humanoid within 5 ft must succeed on a DC 13 Charisma save or be possessed; the ghost controls the body until the host drops to 0 hit points or is turned.

**Tactics.** Talks first. Most Tropelandian ghosts have a grievance and will air it.
