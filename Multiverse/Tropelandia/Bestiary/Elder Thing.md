# Elder Thing

*Large aberration, chaotic neutral.* CR 9. **AC** 15, **HP** 135 (18d10+36), **Speed** 30 ft, swim 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 20 (+5) | 11 (+0) | 15 (+2) | 19 (+4) | 14 (+2) | 10 (+0) |

What the miners of Karak-Dum-Dum were invited down to meet.

## Traits
- **Telepathy.** 120 ft.
- **Amphibious.** Breathes air and water.
- **Resistances.** Psychic; immune to being charmed or frightened.

## Actions
- **Multiattack.** Three tentacle attacks.
- **Tentacle.** Melee, +8 to hit, reach 10 ft. Hit: 10 (3d6) psychic and the target is grappled (escape DC 16).
- **Unspeakable Insight (recharge 5-6).** Each creature within 30 ft that can see it must succeed on a DC 16 Intelligence save or take 22 (5d8) psychic damage and be stunned until the end of its next turn.

**Tactics.** Does not want the party. Wants to be asked a question nobody has asked in a thousand years.
