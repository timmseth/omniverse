# Stone Golem

*Large construct, unaligned.* CR 10. **AC** 17, **HP** 178 (17d10+85), **Speed** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 22 (+6) | 9 (-1) | 20 (+5) | 3 (-4) | 11 (+0) | 1 (-5) |

The Office's security. Carved with the words NO ENTRY WITHOUT APPOINTMENT.

## Traits
- **Immutable Form.** Immune to any spell or effect that would alter its form.
- **Magic Resistance.** Advantage on saves against spells.
- **Immunities.** Poison, psychic; non-magical weapons that are not adamantine; cannot be charmed, exhausted, frightened, paralysed, petrified or poisoned.

## Actions
- **Multiattack.** Two slam attacks.
- **Slam.** Melee, +10 to hit, reach 5 ft. Hit: 19 (3d8+6) bludgeoning.
- **Slow (recharge 5-6).** Each creature of the golem's choice within 10 ft must succeed on a DC 17 Wisdom save or be slowed for 1 minute (halved speed, one attack, no reactions).

**Tactics.** Guards the vault door. Explains nothing.
