# Green Slime Vat

*Large ooze, unaligned.* CR 2. **AC** 8, **HP** 45 (6d10+12), **Speed** 10 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 14 (+2) | 6 (-2) | 14 (+2) | 1 (-5) | 6 (-2) | 2 (-4) |

An experiment Marlowe stopped feeding, which did not stop it.

## Traits
- **Amorphous.** Can move through a 1-inch gap.
- **Corrosive Form.** A creature that touches it or hits it with a melee attack while within 5 ft takes 3 (1d6) acid.

## Actions
- **Pseudopod.** Melee, +5 to hit, reach 5 ft. Hit: 7 (2d6) acid; non-magical metal armour hit is corroded (-1 AC, cumulative).
- **Engulf.** A creature grappled by the slime takes 10 (3d6) acid at the start of each of the slime's turns.

**Tactics.** Overflows the vat when disturbed. Follows metal.
