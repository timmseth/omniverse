# Giant Rat

*Small beast, unaligned.* CR 0.125. **AC** 12, **HP** 7 (2d6), **Speed** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 7 (-2) | 15 (+2) | 11 (+0) | 2 (-4) | 10 (+0) | 4 (-3) |

The rats of Barrowdale are organised, literate and under the impression that they are employees.

## Traits
- **Keen Smell.** Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on smell.
- **Pack Tactics.** Advantage on attack rolls against a creature if at least one ally is within 5 ft of it.

## Actions
- **Bite.** Melee, +4 to hit, reach 5 ft. Hit: 4 (1d4+2) piercing.

**Tactics.** Swarm the nearest ankle. Flee when half the pack is down, unless the Rat King is watching.
