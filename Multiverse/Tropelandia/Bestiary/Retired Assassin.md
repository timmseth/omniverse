# Retired Assassin

*Medium humanoid (any), any evil (retired).* CR 8. **AC** 15, **HP** 78 (12d8+24), **Speed** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 11 (+0) | 16 (+3) | 14 (+2) | 13 (+1) | 11 (+0) | 10 (+0) |

The Society's pensioner-in-residence. Asked to be left out of it. Was not.

## Traits
- **Assassinate.** Advantage on attack rolls against any creature that has not yet acted; a hit against a surprised creature is a critical hit.
- **Evasion.** Takes no damage on a successful Dexterity save against an area effect, half on a failure.
- **Sneak Attack (1/turn).** +14 (4d6) damage with advantage or an ally adjacent.

## Actions
- **Multiattack.** Two shortsword attacks.
- **Poisoned Shortsword.** Melee, +6 to hit, reach 5 ft. Hit: 6 (1d6+3) piercing plus 24 (7d6) poison; DC 15 Constitution save halves the poison damage.
- **Light Crossbow.** Ranged, +6 to hit, range 80/320 ft. Hit: 7 (1d8+3) piercing plus 24 (7d6) poison, save as above.

**Tactics.** Prefers tea. Will kill the rude one first, as a courtesy to the others.
