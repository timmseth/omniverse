# Myconid Sovereign

*Large plant, lawful neutral.* CR 2. **AC** 13, **HP** 60 (8d10+16), **Speed** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 12 (+1) | 10 (+0) | 14 (+2) | 13 (+1) | 15 (+2) | 10 (+0) |

Rules the lower halls of the Folly with a quiet, fungal patience.

## Traits
- **Distress Spores.** When it takes damage, all myconids within 240 ft can sense it.
- **Sun Sickness.** Disadvantage on all rolls in sunlight; dies after 1 hour of it.

## Actions
- **Multiattack.** Two fist attacks.
- **Fist.** Melee, +3 to hit, reach 10 ft. Hit: 5 (1d6+2) bludgeoning plus 7 (3d4) poison.
- **Hallucination Spores (recharge 5-6).** A 10 ft cone; DC 12 Constitution save or poisoned for 1 minute, seeing things.
- **Animating Spores (3/day).** Targets a dead humanoid or beast within 5 ft; it rises as a spore servant in 24 hours.

**Tactics.** Offers tea. The tea is spores. Fights only if the tea is refused rudely.
