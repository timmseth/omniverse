# Rat Sommelier

*Small beast, neutral.* CR 0.25. **AC** 13, **HP** 9 (2d6+2), **Speed** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 7 (-2) | 16 (+3) | 12 (+1) | 10 (+0) | 13 (+1) | 14 (+2) |

Keeper of the innkeeper's wine, which the innkeeper does not know he still has.

## Traits
- **Keen Smell.** As giant rat, and can identify any vintage by it.
- **Nimble Escape.** Disengage or Hide as a bonus action.

## Actions
- **Bite.** Melee, +4 to hit, reach 5 ft. Hit: 4 (1d4+2) piercing; disadvantage if the party has been polite about the wine.
- **Recommend.** One creature within 10 ft must succeed on a DC 11 Wisdom save or spend its next action tasting.

**Tactics.** Guards the cellar's good bottles. Will trade.
