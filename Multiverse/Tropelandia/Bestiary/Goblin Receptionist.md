# Goblin Receptionist

*Small humanoid (goblinoid), lawful neutral.* CR 0.25. **AC** 15, **HP** 7 (2d6), **Speed** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8 (-1) | 14 (+2) | 10 (+0) | 12 (+1) | 10 (+0) | 12 (+1) |

Every union hall has one. Nobody has ever got past one by asking nicely.

## Traits
- **Nimble Escape.** Can Disengage or Hide as a bonus action.
- **Front Desk.** Advantage on Insight checks against anyone without an appointment.

## Actions
- **Pencil.** Melee, +4 to hit, reach 5 ft. Hit: 4 (1d4+2) piercing.
- **Take a Number.** One creature within 30 ft must succeed on a DC 11 Wisdom save or spend its next action waiting.

**Tactics.** Does not fight unless the desk is touched. Then fights for the desk.
