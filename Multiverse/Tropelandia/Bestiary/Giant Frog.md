# Giant Frog

*Medium beast, unaligned.* CR 0.25. **AC** 11, **HP** 18 (4d8), **Speed** 30 ft, swim 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 12 (+1) | 13 (+1) | 11 (+0) | 2 (-4) | 10 (+0) | 3 (-4) |

The marsh frogs have learned hymns. They are not good at them.

## Traits
- **Amphibious.** Breathes air and water.
- **Standing Leap.** Long jump up to 20 ft and high jump up to 10 ft, with or without a running start.

## Actions
- **Bite.** Melee, +3 to hit, reach 5 ft. Hit: 4 (1d6+1) piercing and the target is grappled (escape DC 11) and restrained.
- **Swallow.** One Small or smaller creature it is grappling: 5 (2d4) acid damage at the start of each of the frog's turns while swallowed.

**Tactics.** Leap from the reeds, swallow the smallest, retreat into the water.
