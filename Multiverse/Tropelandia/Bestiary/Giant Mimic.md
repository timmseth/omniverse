# Giant Mimic

*Huge monstrosity (shapechanger), neutral.* CR 9. **AC** 12, **HP** 180 (19d12+57), **Speed** 10 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 21 (+5) | 10 (+0) | 17 (+3) | 7 (-2) | 14 (+2) | 10 (+0) |

The Roadside Welcome, Whisperwood's finest inn.

## Traits
- **Shapechanger.** Can take the form of a building or a room of one.
- **Adhesive.** As mimic; the whole floor counts.
- **False Appearance.** Indistinguishable from an ordinary inn while motionless.
- **Regeneration.** Regains 10 hit points at the start of its turn while it has at least one guest inside.

## Actions
- **Multiattack.** Two pseudopod attacks and one bite.
- **Pseudopod.** Melee, +9 to hit, reach 15 ft. Hit: 16 (2d10+5) bludgeoning and the target is adhered (grappled, escape DC 17).
- **Bite.** Melee, +9 to hit, reach 10 ft. Hit: 16 (2d10+5) piercing plus 13 (3d8) acid.
- **Swallow Room.** The giant mimic reshapes; each creature inside its object form must succeed on a DC 17 Dexterity save or be restrained by walls that are teeth.

**Tactics.** Does not attack until the party has paid. It is a business.
