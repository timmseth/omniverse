# Lich

*Medium undead, lawful evil.* CR 21. **AC** 17, **HP** 135 (18d8+54), **Speed** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 11 (+0) | 16 (+3) | 16 (+3) | 20 (+5) | 14 (+2) | 16 (+3) |

Lord Vexmort found immortality easy and paperwork hard, and has had eleven centuries to confuse the two.

## Traits
- **Rejuvenation.** If it has a phylactery, a destroyed lich gains a new body in 1d10 days.
- **Legendary Resistance (3/day).** May choose to succeed on a failed save.
- **Turn Resistance.** Advantage on saves against being turned.
- **Resistances.** Cold, lightning, necrotic; immune to poison and non-magical weapons.

## Actions
- **Paralysing Touch.** Melee spell, +12 to hit, reach 5 ft. Hit: 10 (3d6) cold; DC 18 Constitution save or paralysed for 1 minute (repeat the save at the end of each turn).
- **Spellcasting (DC 20, +12).** Cantrips: mage hand, prestidigitation, ray of frost. 1st-9th level slots. Signature: counterspell, dimension door, disintegrate, dominate monster, finger of death, globe of invulnerability, power word kill, power word stun.
- **Legendary Actions (3/round).** Cantrip; Paralysing Touch (2 actions); Frightening Gaze (2 actions, DC 18 Wisdom or frightened 1 minute); Disrupt Life (3 actions, 21 (6d6) necrotic to each non-undead within 20 ft, DC 18 Constitution halves).

**Tactics.** Would rather not. Asks, once, for the form. Then disintegrates the form.
