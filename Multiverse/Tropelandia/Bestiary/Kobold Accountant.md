# Kobold Accountant

*Small humanoid (kobold), lawful neutral.* CR 0.25. **AC** 12, **HP** 5 (2d6-2), **Speed** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 7 (-2) | 15 (+2) | 9 (-1) | 14 (+2) | 12 (+1) | 10 (+0) |

The backbone of Glintfang Holdings.

## Traits
- **Pack Tactics.** Advantage on attack rolls against a creature if an ally is within 5 ft of it.
- **Exact Change.** Knows the value of any coin, gem or art object on sight.

## Actions
- **Pen.** Melee, +3 to hit, reach 5 ft. Hit: 2 (1d4) piercing.
- **Audit.** One creature within 30 ft must succeed on a DC 12 Intelligence save or have disadvantage on its next attack roll while it recounts its coins.

**Tactics.** Does not want to fight. Wants a receipt.
