# Mimic

*Medium monstrosity (shapechanger), neutral.* CR 2. **AC** 12, **HP** 58 (9d8+18), **Speed** 15 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 17 (+3) | 12 (+1) | 15 (+2) | 5 (-3) | 13 (+1) | 8 (-1) |

Tropelandia's mimics have gone into hospitality.

## Traits
- **Shapechanger.** Can take the form of an object or revert to its amorphous form.
- **Adhesive.** Adheres to anything that touches it while in object form; a Huge or smaller creature adhered is grappled (escape DC 13).
- **False Appearance.** Indistinguishable from an ordinary object while motionless.
- **Grappler.** Advantage on attack rolls against any creature grappled by it.

## Actions
- **Pseudopod.** Melee, +5 to hit, reach 5 ft. Hit: 7 (1d8+3) bludgeoning; if the mimic is in object form, the target is adhered.
- **Bite.** Melee, +5 to hit, reach 5 ft. Hit: 7 (1d8+3) piercing plus 4 (1d8) acid.

**Tactics.** Is a chair until it is not a chair.
