# Swarm of Rats

*Medium swarm of tiny beasts, unaligned.* CR 0.25. **AC** 10, **HP** 24 (7d8-7), **Speed** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 9 (-1) | 11 (+0) | 9 (-1) | 2 (-4) | 10 (+0) | 3 (-4) |



## Traits
- **Swarm.** Can occupy another creature's space and move through any gap a Tiny rat could. Cannot regain hit points or gain temporary hit points.
- **Keen Smell.** Advantage on Perception checks that rely on smell.

## Actions
- **Bites.** Melee, +2 to hit, reach 0 ft. Hit: 7 (2d6) piercing, or 3 (1d6) if the swarm is at half hit points or fewer.

**Tactics.** Flow over whoever is carrying food.
