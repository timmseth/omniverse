# Young Gold Dragon

*Large dragon, lawful good (fiscally).* CR 10. **AC** 18, **HP** 178 (17d10+85), **Speed** 40 ft, fly 80 ft, swim 40 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 23 (+6) | 14 (+2) | 21 (+5) | 16 (+3) | 13 (+1) | 20 (+5) |

Glintfang, proprietor of Glintfang Holdings, who found that a hoard grows faster if you lend it.

## Traits
- **Amphibious.** Breathes air and water.
- **Immunities.** Fire.
- **Change Shape (Glintfang).** Can polymorph into a humanoid; uses the mage stat block in that form.

## Actions
- **Multiattack.** One bite and two claw attacks.
- **Bite.** Melee, +10 to hit, reach 10 ft. Hit: 17 (2d10+6) piercing.
- **Claw.** Melee, +10 to hit, reach 5 ft. Hit: 13 (2d6+6) slashing.
- **Breath Weapons (recharge 5-6).** Fire Breath: 30 ft cone, DC 17 Dexterity save, 55 (10d10) fire, half on a save. Weakening Breath: 30 ft cone, DC 17 Strength save or disadvantage on Strength-based rolls for 1 minute.

**Tactics.** Negotiates. Breathes fire only on anyone who touches the portfolio without signing in.
