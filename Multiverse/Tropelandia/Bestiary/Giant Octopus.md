# Giant Octopus

*Large beast, unaligned.* CR 1. **AC** 11, **HP** 52 (8d10+8), **Speed** 10 ft, swim 60 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 17 (+3) | 13 (+1) | 13 (+1) | 4 (-3) | 10 (+0) | 4 (-3) |

It came up the drowned shaft of Karak-Dum-Dum and liked the ale.

## Traits
- **Hold Breath.** Can hold its breath for 1 hour out of water.
- **Underwater Camouflage.** Advantage on Stealth checks underwater.

## Actions
- **Tentacles.** Melee, +5 to hit, reach 15 ft. Hit: 10 (2d6+3) bludgeoning; the target is grappled (escape DC 16) and restrained.
- **Ink Cloud (recharges after a rest).** A 20 ft radius cloud of ink underwater; the area is heavily obscured for 1 minute.

**Tactics.** Grab from the flooded dark, drag toward the deep.
