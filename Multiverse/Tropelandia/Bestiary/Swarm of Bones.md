# Swarm of Bones

*Large swarm of tiny undead, neutral evil.* CR 1. **AC** 11, **HP** 30 (4d10+8), **Speed** 20 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 12 (+1) | 13 (+1) | 14 (+2) | 2 (-4) | 8 (-1) | 3 (-4) |

The chapel's ossuary, awake.

## Traits
- **Swarm.** As swarm of rats.

- **Rattle.** Melee, +4 to hit, reach 0 ft. Hit: 7 (2d6) bludgeoning, or 3 (1d6) at half hit points or fewer.

**Tactics.** Rolls across the floor like gravel with intent.
