# The Counting Thing

*Large aberration.* Threat 100.

It came through the door in the Cold Vault. It is polite, patient and from somewhere else, and it wants the dead of Ashfall filed.

| Body | Wits | Nerve | Grace | Harm | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | 5 | 4 | 2 | 5 | 3 |

## Moves
- **Number You.** Nerve 4 against the target's Nerve; if it wins, the target knows exactly how many days they have left, and takes two harm from knowing.
- **Take a Number.** Hands the target a paper with a queue number. While they hold it, nothing in the dark will harm them. They cannot put it down.

**Tactics.** Does not attack. Offers a place in a queue. Attacks only those who refuse the paper.
