# The Vault Opens

*Year 211, day 44.*

The iron door is found open at dawn. Kettle goes in and comes out with a coin and a paper. The coin has a tiny signature. This is the Ashfall end of the Rift that the Office of Eternal Records in Tropelandia has been filing through for two centuries.
