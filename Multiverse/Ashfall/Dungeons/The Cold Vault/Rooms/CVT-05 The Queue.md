# CVT-05 The Queue

> A long warm corridor lit by a light that is not fire. A rope on brass posts runs its length. Along it, more of the dead, each holding a grey paper, each shuffling one step forward every few minutes toward an archway at the far end. A sign on the wall reads NOW SERVING, and a number. The number has not changed in two hundred years.
