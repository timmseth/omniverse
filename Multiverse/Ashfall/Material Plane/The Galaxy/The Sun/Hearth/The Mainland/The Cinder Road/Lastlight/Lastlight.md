# Lastlight

> A ring of stone houses around a great fire-pit that has not gone out in two hundred years. Lamps hang from every eave. Ash lies on the roofs like grey snow, and beyond the last lamp the plain is simply black.

The last town on the Cinder Road, named in hope and kept in stubbornness. Six hundred people, one fire, and the Wardens who walk the lamp-line every Watchnight counting what is still burning.
