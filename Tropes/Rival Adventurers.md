# Rival Adventurers

Two people of similar skill and very different temperaments who cannot stop competing. The competition makes both of them better and neither of them happier.

## Instances
- [[1489-0034 The Pie Contest Incident]] is where [[Marlowe the Mildly Menacing]] and [[Dame Rosalind Quickblade]] became rivals.
